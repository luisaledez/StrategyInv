## Key Question 1: Quarterly numbers and beat/miss for all 16 quarters (GAAP vs non-GAAP)

### Takeaway
Insulet beat consensus adjusted EPS in 15 of 16 quarters (only miss: Q2 2024, $0.55 vs ~$0.56) and beat consensus revenue in all 16; it raised full-year revenue guidance at every quarterly report from Q3 2022 through Q1 2026 and cut it for the first time at Q2 2026 (Aug 5, 2026). GAAP net income is distorted by a $146.9M (Q2 2024) + $14.8M (Q3 2024) + Q4 tranche valuation-allowance release (FY2024 total tax benefit $190.8M VA release + $8.3M R&D credit recovery per the Q4 2024 release; the Q4 2025 release restates the 2024 items as "totaling $191.3M") and by $123.9M of losses on debt extinguishment in FY2025 (of which $84.4M in Q2 2025).

### Inferences
- Insulet's "beat and raise" cadence was unbroken for 14 consecutive quarters (Q3 2022–Q1 2026); Q2 2026 was the first guidance cut in this window, which explains the disproportionate -20% reaction to a numerically small (100 bp) total-company cut.
- GAAP EPS is not comparable across years: FY2024 GAAP EPS $5.78 is inflated by ~$199M of tax benefits (adj EPS $3.24), and FY2025 GAAP EPS $3.48 is depressed by $123.9M of debt-extinguishment losses (adj EPS $4.97). The local cache's "trailing P/E 104x on 2025-09-12" is computed on a GAAP TTM EPS of ~$3.43 that is depressed by these charges; on TTM adjusted EPS (~$4.24 at that date: $0.90+$1.15+$1.02+$1.17) the peak multiple was ~83x.

### Gaps
- Sell-side (non-Zacks) consensus for Q3 2022–Q1 2024 was not found; only Zacks surprise percentages are available.
- Adjusted gross margin and adjusted operating margin were not separately disclosed for 2024 quarters; Q1 2024 adjusted EPS not confirmed as distinct from GAAP.
- Q4 2025 adjusted EBITDA is derived, not disclosed.
