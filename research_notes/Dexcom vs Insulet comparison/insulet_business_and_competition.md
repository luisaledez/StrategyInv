# Insulet (PODD): Business Model, Competitive Position, Growth Drivers and Risks (as of September 27, 2026)

Scope note: all figures are dated. "cc" = constant currency. Company-reported figures come from Insulet's 8-K earnings releases, 10-Q/10-K filings and earnings-call transcripts; third-party figures are labelled. Where sources conflict, both are given.

## Business model: pod economics, customers, new starts, margins, manufacturing, drug delivery

### Takeaway
Insulet sells a disposable, tubeless 3-day pod through the US pharmacy channel (no upfront durable, Medicare Part D rather than Part B DME), which drove FY2025 revenue of $2.7B (+30.7%) at a 71.6% gross margin and >600,000 active global users. The model's weak point surfaced in Q2 2026: Type 2 customers (now >40% of US new starts) churn heavily in their first 90 days, forcing a cut to US growth guidance and a ~22% one-day stock drop on August 5, 2026.

### Inferences
- Revenue per active user: FY2025 Omnipod revenue of $2.674B over ~600k users implies roughly $4,400-4,800 per user per year (~$370-400/month, blended US/international, and overstated to the extent the 600k is a year-end figure). This is consistent with the ~$820 list per 10-pod box net of rebates/discounts. (Analyst estimate, not company-disclosed.)
- The US deceleration is stark: US Omnipod growth went 28.3% (Q1 2026) -> 20.1% (Q2) -> guided 14-16% (Q3). The guidance implies Q4 US growth in the low-to-mid teens, so the "mid-teens exit" becomes the 2027 base case before any retention fixes.
- Because >85% of starts are MDI conversions and Type 2 is >40% of starts, Insulet's growth is now more dependent on onboarding first-time pump users with primary-care prescribers and Medicare/Medicaid coverage — a structurally higher-churn cohort than the Type 1 endocrinology base.

### Gaps
- Insulet does not disclose US vs international active-customer split, Type 2 active-customer counts, or quantified retention/attrition rates (only "meaningfully lower" for Type 2 in the first 90 days). No third-party figures found.
- Annual new-customer-start counts for 2023-2026 are not disclosed; only "record" and "second-highest" qualitative statements.
- Gross-margin drivers by site (Acton vs Malaysia mix) were not found.

## Market: penetration, size, share of new starts, international expansion

### Takeaway
Insulet frames the pump market as ~40% penetrated in US Type 1, ~25% in international Type 1 and <5% in US insulin-using Type 2, with a ~$30B global TAM; it claims #1 in US and European new starts and #1 prescribed AID in the US, and international is now the faster-growing segment (Omnipod 5 in 20 countries, guided +30-32% cc for 2026).

### Inferences
- Rough US new-start share triangulation: Tandem's 22k US Q2 shipments include replacements/renewals, and Beta Bionics is small (Q2 revenue $32M). Given Insulet's 23% customer-base growth on a >600k base (~140k+ net adds annualised before churn), Insulet very likely remains the largest source of US new pump starts in 2026, but no independent share figure was found.
- International is a Type 1-dominated, Omnipod 5-upgrade-driven story (only >70% on Omnipod 5 internationally vs >95% in US), so international growth should be less exposed to the Type 2 churn problem in 2026-2027.

### Gaps

- International Type 2 launch status for Omnipod 5 was not confirmed (Investor Day implies international Type 2 expansion is post-2028).

## Type 2 opportunity: clearance, SECURE-T2D, coverage, adoption pace, competition

### Takeaway
Omnipod 5 has been FDA-cleared for Type 2 (18+) since August 2024 on the strength of SECURE-T2D (A1c -0.8 pts, +~5 h/day time-in-range), and Type 2 grew to >40% of US new starts by Q2 2026; but first-90-day retention and utilization are "meaningfully lower" than Type 1, and Insulet cut 2026 US guidance and re-tooled its commercial model around a 45-day retention metric. Tandem (Control-IQ+ cleared for Type 2 early 2025) reports stable Type 2 attrition "only modestly higher" than Type 1.

### Cited Findings
- FDA cleared Omnipod 5 for Type 2 (ages 18+) in August 2024, the first AID cleared for both Type 1 and Type 2. — [Insulet Q4/FY2024 8-K Ex. 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1145197/000114519725000006/podd202412-31ex991.htm)
- SECURE-T2D (presented ADA June 2024): 305 adults aged 18-75 on insulin (basal-bolus, premix or basal-only), A1c <12%; A1c fell from 8.2% to 7.4% at 13 weeks (treatment effect -0.8 pts); -2.1 pts for baseline A1c >=9% (10.1% to 8.1%); ~5 more hours/day in range (70-180 mg/dL) with no increase in hypoglycemia; mean age 57, 24% Black, 22% Hispanic/Latino. — [Patient Care Online](https://www.patientcareonline.com/view/secure-t2d-automated-insulin-delivery-system-reduced-a1c-in-adults-with-type-2-diabetes); [Insulet press release, June 2024](https://investors.insulet.com/news/news-details/2024/Insulets-SECURE-T2D-Pivotal-Trial-Results-Demonstrate-Omnipod-5-Improves-Clinical-Outcomes-and-Quality-of-Life-in-Type-2-Diabetes/default.aspx)
- Adoption pace: Type 2 >30% of US new starts in Q1 2025; "over 40%" in Q2 2026. — [Quartr Q1 2025](https://quartr.com/events/insulet-corporation-podd-q1-2025_ozsR3TfF); [Motley Fool Q2 2026 call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/insulet-podd-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Retention problem (Q2 2026 call): "We are seeing lower rates of utilization and retention among type 2 customers, and these trends were more pronounced this quarter than we anticipated"; "Once type 2 customers remain on Omnipod for the first 90 days, retention rates stabilize." CEO McEvoy said management "should have identified the type 2 retention issue sooner" and identified ~10 pain points in the first 90 days (e.g., first pod change, first refill). Root causes cited: slower disease progression, more comorbidities, ~60% Medicare/Medicaid payer mix (vs ~40% for Type 1), primary-care-dominated prescribers. — [Motley Fool Q2 2026 call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/insulet-podd-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/); [Investing.com, Q2 2026 slides](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/insulet-q2-2026-slides-show-growth-amid-retention-challenges-93CH-4837815); [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/US_PODD_2026-08-05)
- Remediation (Q3 2026 onward): sales compensation restructured to reward retention at 45 days rather than only new starts; expanded customer-care teams for insurance navigation and higher-touch onboarding; refined sampling to target patients most likely to benefit; scaling the Omnipod Discover cloud platform (>12,000 patients and 1,600 HCPs by Q2 2026, with early data suggesting a retention benefit). — [Motley Fool Q2 2026 call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/insulet-podd-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Coverage: in Q2 2026 Insulet "added coverage for 6.5 million lives" and "simplified prior authorization requirements for approximately 10 million lives"; some plans require annual re-authorization. — [Motley Fool Q2 2026 call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/insulet-podd-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/); [GoodRx Medicare coverage](https://www.goodrx.com/omnipod-5/medicare-coverage)
- Type 2 pipeline: Omnipod 6 (adaptive learning algorithm, broader CGM compatibility) for Type 1 and Type 2 launching 2027 (STRIVE pivotal data at ADA 2026 showed improved time-in-range with "significantly less bolusing"); first fully closed-loop Type 2 system (no setup or meal entry) targeted for 2028, EVOLVE IDE pivotal enrolling since Q1 2026 with regulatory submission planned 2027, aimed at primary-care patients; EVOLUTION 3 feasibility data at ADA 2026. — [Drug Delivery Business, ADA 2026](https://www.drugdeliverybusiness.com/biggest-diabetes-tech-news-ada-2026/); [Insulet Q1 2026 8-K Ex. 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001145197/000114519726000100/podd2026-03x31ex991.htm); [Motley Fool Q2 2026 call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/insulet-podd-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Competition in Type 2: Tandem's Control-IQ+ received FDA Type 2 clearance in early 2025 and an expanded CE mark for Type 2 and pregnancy in 2026; Tandem says Type 2 attrition "has remained stable over the past five years and is only modestly higher than type 1 rates," with ~70% of its new starts from MDI. ## GLP-1 impact on pump demand

### Takeaway
Insulet's position since 2023 is that GLP-1s do not shrink its addressable market and are complementary (nearly half of its Type 2 feasibility-trial enrollees used GLP-1s plus insulin); its 10-K nonetheless lists GLP-1s delaying Type 2 progression as a risk, and management did not cite GLP-1s as a cause of the 2026 Type 2 retention shortfall.

### Cited Findings
- Then-CEO Jim Hollingshead (August 2023) said the latest GLP-1s would not affect Insulet's TAM, citing rising diabetes prevalence; ~2.5M US Type 2 patients use MDI with single-digit pump penetration; nearly half of enrollees in Insulet's Type 2 feasibility trial were on both GLP-1s and insulin. — [MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/insulin-pump-glp-1s-diabetes-devices/693600/)
- Analyst physician surveys were skeptical GLP-1s change the long-term diabetes-device outlook (the Type 1 study that spooked stocks had 10 newly diagnosed patients). — [MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/insulin-pump-glp-1s-diabetes-devices/693600/); [Drug Delivery Business](https://www.drugdeliverybusiness.com/analysts-glp-1s-minor-impact-insulin-pump/)
- Investor Day (Nov 2025): SECURE-T2D positioned as showing "complementary (not competitive)" positioning with GLP-1s. — [Drug Delivery Business, Investor Day](https://www.drugdeliverybusiness.com/insulet-ceo-ashley-mcevoy-omnipod-6-innovation-roadmap/)
- 10-K risk factor cites "increasing use of GLP-1 products that may delay the progression of type 2 diabetes." — [Insulet FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1145197/000114519726000028/podd-20251231.htm)
- The Q2 2026 call contained no GLP-1 discussion; the retention causes cited were disease progression, comorbidities, payer mix and prescriber type. — [Motley Fool Q2 2026 call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/insulet-podd-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/); [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/US_PODD_2026-08-05)

### Inferences
- The observable 2026 pressure on Insulet's Type 2 franchise is churn, not a lack of starts, so GLP-1 substitution is not evident in the data; a slower-progressing Type 2 pool on GLP-1s could, however, mean lower pod utilization per user over time (management did flag "utilization" as well as retention).

### Gaps
- No Insulet or third-party quantitative evidence (e.g., share of Omnipod Type 2 users on GLP-1s in 2025-2026, or GLP-1 discontinuation-driven churn) was found for 2025-2026.
