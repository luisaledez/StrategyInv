## 3. Earnings quality and headline adjustments

- **Q3 2025 GAAP EPS $0.70 vs non-GAAP $0.61:** the difference is **$82.7M of income from equity investments** (excluded from non-GAAP), partly offset by $22.7M business-transition items and $8.0M amortization, and $11.0M of tax adjustments; GAAP net income $283.8M vs non-GAAP $242.5M; GAAP diluted shares 407.4M vs non-GAAP 399.7M ([Q3 2025 8-K Ex. 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1093557/000109355725000278/dxcm09302025-exhibit991.htm)). **Correction to the deep dive**, whose Table A note attributes GAAP > non-GAAP in Q3 2025 to discrete tax benefits; the release shows it was the equity-investment gain. (The Q4 2023 and Q1 2024 attributions were not re-checked.)
- Q2 2026: non-GAAP net income $269.1M excludes $9.4M amortization, $0.6M business-transition items and $10.0M loss from equity investments; no tax adjustment in Q2 (H1: $7.7M) (Q2 2026 8-K Table D).
- **Stock comp is not excluded from Dexcom's non-GAAP EPS**; it is only added back in adjusted EBITDA ($39.0M Q2 2026, $82.3M H1 2026; $159.6M FY2025, $170.4M FY2024, $150.8M FY2023). Unrecognized RSU/PSU cost $263.6M over ~2.2 years (10-Q). FCF (OCF - capex) of $1,405M TTM includes the stock-comp add-back; FCF minus ~$165M of stock comp ≈ $1.24B (derived).
- **Tax:** 2025 GAAP effective rate 23.2% (tax $252.1M on pretax $1,088.4M, 10-K); estimated 2026 annual rate 21.8%, H1 2026 actual 23.6% (share-based-compensation shortfalls net of disallowed executive compensation); the decline is attributed to the start of a Malaysia tax holiday (10-Q). Derived non-GAAP rate: H1 2026 non-GAAP operating income $592.7M + net interest $30.3M = $623.0M pretax vs non-GAAP net income $485.4M → **~22%**. The deep dive's scenario note assumed a 17-18% non-GAAP rate; the reported numbers do not support that, and the thesis scenarios use ~21.5%.
- **Other income is shrinking:** 2025 other income $176.6M (16% of pretax), mostly interest on ~$3B of cash; after the $1.21B note repayment and buybacks, interest income fell to $16.9M a quarter. This is why TTM net income / operating income is 0.88 (v3 guard).
- **Convertible dilution:** H1 2026 basic 383.7M, RSU/PSU dilution 0.4M, convertible notes (if-converted) 7.7M → diluted 391.8M; Q2 2026 basic 382.2M, diluted 390.1M. Q2 non-GAAP EPS $0.70 on $269.1M implies ~384M non-GAAP shares (derived), i.e. non-GAAP excludes the note shares as in Q3 2025. Cash settlement in May 2028 would remove ~7.7M shares (~2%) from the GAAP diluted count.
- **TTM roll-off:** point-in-time TTM GAAP EPS $2.53 = Q3 2025 $0.70 + Q4 2025 $0.68 + Q1 2026 $0.51 + Q2 2026 $0.64 (edgar cache). With the Q3 2026 print, the $0.70 quarter leaves. Third-party Q3 2026 consensus non-GAAP EPS $0.66 ([MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/NASDAQ/DXCM/earnings/), estimated report date Oct 29, not confirmed); GAAP would be a few cents lower after amortization, so TTM GAAP EPS likely prints ~$2.45-2.50, flat to slightly down despite growth. The $2.15 trigger needs Q3 2026 GAAP EPS of $0.32 or less (derived: $2.15 - $1.83).
