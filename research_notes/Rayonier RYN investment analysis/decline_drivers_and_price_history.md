## Key Question 2: Dated timeline of RYN's major price legs and attached causes

### Takeaway
RYN's decline is a five-leg story: (1) a 2022 rate-shock/lumber-crash leg from $45.87 to ~$30; (2) an Oct 2023 leg to $24.84 as the 10-year hit 5% and PNW/New Zealand EBITDA collapsed; (3) a 2024 leg driven by weak Southern pulpwood, guidance cuts, the $495M Washington/Oklahoma sale and a $1.80 special paid mostly in stock; (4) a 2025 leg (tariff shock, Q1 miss, Helene salvage, mill closures, New Zealand exit, then the PotlatchDeltic merger announcement and $1.40 special) taking the stock from $28.31 to ~$22; and (5) a 2026 leg from ~$24.82 to $18.88 as the merged company traded down on flat Southern timber outlook, a Q2 EPS miss, chairman selling, and a September 2026 bond rout (10-year >5%, Fed hike, mortgages >7%).

### Inferences
- The two sharpest single-month drops — Dec 2024 (-18.1% from $31.87 to $26.10) and Oct 2025 (-16.8% from $26.54 to $22.07) — each coincided with a special dividend paid ~75% in new shares ($1.80 and $1.40 respectively). A meaningful portion of those declines is mechanical (the ex-dividend adjustment plus dilution), not a repricing of the business; on the other hand Oct 2025 also absorbed the merger announcement, which the market treated as negative for RYN (RYN -8.5% from $25.44 to $23.27 within nine trading days, while PCH rose 5% on day one).
- The 2026 leg happened without a headline earnings collapse (Adjusted EBITDA rose with the merger). The drivers appear to be (a) "flat to down" Southern timber outlook and no pulpwood recovery timeline, (b) higher rates/REIT de-rating (Sept 2026 bond rout), (c) integration/leverage skepticism, and (d) a ~140.9M-share increase in float that changes the holder base (former PCH holders, including the chairman, selling).
- The Sept 24, 2026 -4% day lines up with the 10-year crossing 5% and the mortgage rate crossing 7%, consistent with rate sensitivity; WY fell a similar amount in September (see KQ3).
