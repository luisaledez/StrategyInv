# Rayonier Inc. (NYSE: RYN) — Business, Segments, Results, Balance Sheet, Capital Allocation and Guidance (as of late September 2026)

Research date: 2026-09-27. Most recent reported quarter: Q2 2026 (period ended 2026-06-30; released 2026-08-05; 10-Q filed 2026-08-07). No Q3 2026 results or Q3 release-date announcement were found as of 2026-09-27 (see Gaps). All figures dated inline.

**Headline change vs. the "standalone timber REIT" framing in the assignment:** Rayonier is no longer a pure three-segment timberland REIT. On 2026-01-30 it closed an all-stock "merger of equals" with PotlatchDeltic (PCH). The combined company kept the Rayonier name and RYN ticker, now owns ~4.1M acres, and reports four segments: Southern Timber, Northwest Timber (renamed from Pacific Northwest Timber), Wood Products (new — 6 sawmills + 1 plywood mill), and Real Estate. New Zealand was sold 2025-06-30 and is reported as discontinued operations. Details and primary sources below.

## Key Question 1: What are Rayonier's segments and how did each perform in the latest 2–3 quarters (volumes, prices, EBITDA)?

### Takeaway
Post-merger Rayonier reports four segments (Southern Timber, Northwest Timber, Wood Products, Real Estate) on ~4.1M acres. Q2 2026 Adjusted EBITDA was $123.7M (vs $44.9M in Q2 2025) with 5 months of PotlatchDeltic contribution; H1 2026 Adjusted EBITDA was $217.8M. Volumes roughly doubled from the merger, but Southern pine prices are down YoY (pulpwood weak), Northwest log prices are up, lumber realizations jumped 18% QoQ to $505/MBF in Q2, and Real Estate is running ahead of guidance.

## Key Question 3: What happened with the New Zealand JV sale, use of proceeds, special dividend, buybacks, and the $1B+ asset disposition program?

### Takeaway
The November 2023 $1B disposition plan finished at ~$1.45B (Nov 2023–Jun 2025), capped by the $710M sale of the 77% New Zealand JV stake to The Rohatyn Group on 2025-06-30 ($699M expected net; $688.3M net cash received; $404.4M gain). Proceeds funded a $1.40/share special dividend (paid 2025-12-12; 25% cash / 75% stock — $54.6M cash), debt reduction and buybacks ($70.5M in 2025; $103.5M in H1 2026 at ~$21/share; $126M authorization left at 2026-06-30).

- Nov 2023 plan: target $1B of select asset sales over 18 months to reduce leverage to ≤3.0x Net Debt/Adjusted EBITDA, exploit the private/public value gap, and return capital — [Rayonier initiatives release (2023-11-01), 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/52827/000005282723000208/initiativespressrelease.htm)
