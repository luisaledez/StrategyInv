## What were revenue, GAAP EPS, non-GAAP EPS, gross margin and operating margin each quarter?

### Takeaway
Revenue rose from $770M (Q3 2022) to $1,308M (Q2 2026), but growth was far from linear: it accelerated to 24-27% through 2023, collapsed to 2% in Q3 2024 after the salesforce/DME/rebate stumble, then re-accelerated to 22% in Q3 2025 and settled at 12-13% organic in 2026. Non-GAAP gross margin fell from ~64-67% (2022-23) to a trough of 57.5% in Q1 2025 (freight, scrap, inventory charge) and has recovered to 64.1% in Q2 2026; non-GAAP operating margin has expanded to a record 25-26% in the last three quarters.

### Inferences
- The GAAP-vs-non-GAAP gross-margin gap widened to ~330 bps in Q3 2024 (59.7% vs 63.0%) because the non-cash inventory charge tied to the business-transition items was excluded from non-GAAP; in every other quarter the gap is 60-110 bps (amortization/stock comp), so the non-GAAP margin series is the cleaner read of underlying manufacturing performance.
- Q1 is structurally the weakest margin quarter (deductible resets, seasonally lower volumes): non-GAAP OM was 10.6%, 15.2%, 13.8% and 22.2% in Q1 2023-2026. The 840 bp jump in Q1 2026 is the clearest evidence that the 2024-25 cost problems have been fixed.

### Gaps
- Q3 2022 non-GAAP gross margin was not separately quantified in the extracted release (the release only discusses GAAP gross profit of 64.2% and ~64% full-year non-GAAP guidance). Not verified.
- Quarterly adjusted EBITDA for Q4 2022, Q4 2023 and Q4 2024 was not extracted (only full-year figures were captured). Could be derived by subtracting the nine-month figures but was not done.

### Inferences
- The consensus EPS figures for 2022-2023 quarters (Zacks) look low relative to the actual prints (e.g., $0.22 vs $0.34 in Q2 2023, $0.33-0.34 vs $0.50 in Q3 2023). Part of this is real (Dexcom was beating heavily during the G7 ramp) but part is a definitional mismatch: Dexcom's non-GAAP EPS in 2023 benefited from a lower non-GAAP tax rate and from convertible-note accounting, so the size of the 2023 EPS beats should not be compared 1:1 with 2025-26 beats.
- Since Q3 2024 the beats have been small and consistent (1-3% on revenue), which is consistent with management guiding conservatively after the July 2024 credibility hit; Q1 and Q2 2026 EPS beats of $0.09 each are the largest since 2023 and were driven by margin, not revenue.

### Gaps
- Consensus revenue for Q2 2023 and Q4 2023 was not captured in any source retrieved (only "beat" was reported). Q1 2023 and Q4 2022 revenue consensus are implied from Zacks' stated surprise percentages, not quoted directly.
- Zacks' Q3 2023 consensus was reported by a search summary as $0.22, but its own "beat by 47.1%" math implies ~$0.34, and Seeking Alpha's preview shows $0.33; the $0.22 figure is treated as a transcription error.
- Consensus figures differ by provider (Zacks vs LSEG/FactSet vs MarketBeat); no single provider covered all 16 quarters.

## How did full-year guidance evolve across 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026?

### Takeaway
FY2023 was a textbook beat-and-raise year (initial $3.35-3.49B raised three times to $3.575-3.60B; actual $3.622B). FY2024 was initially guided at $4.15-4.35B, nudged up in April, then cut to $4.00-4.05B in July 2024 (actual $4.033B, with margins also missing). FY2025 started at $4.60B with GM 64-65%, saw the GM guide cut twice (to 62% in May, 61% in October) while revenue was raised twice (actual $4.662B). FY2026 started at $5.16-5.25B / GM 63-64% / OM 22-23% and has been raised twice on margins and once on revenue to the current $5.18-5.25B / ~64% / 23.5-24%.
